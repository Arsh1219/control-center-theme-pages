The app

Control Center opens a themed control panel over your screen with quick settings, music controls, brightness and volume sliders, shortcuts and a screen recorder. The free version includes 24 themes, the full control panel and the screen recorder, and shows ads. You can unlock more themes one at a time by watching a rewarded video ad. Premium unlocks every theme and the custom theme editor, and removes ads.

Some phone settings, such as WiFi, Bluetooth, mobile data and airplane mode, cannot be switched by apps on current Android versions. For those, the panel shows the current state and opens the matching Android setting. Features can behave differently depending on your phone and Android version.
